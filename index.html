RESPONSIBLE INVESTIGATION

Use OSINT ethically

Authorisation first

Investigate only your own assets, public-interest material, or targets covered by explicit permission and a defined scope.

Verify independently

A username match, DNS record, search result or metadata field is a lead—not proof. Corroborate important findings.

Protect people and data

Minimise personal data collection. Never use exposed credentials or attempt unauthorised access.

Keep evidence traceable

Record source URLs, timestamps, tool versions, collection methods, limitations and confidence levels.
